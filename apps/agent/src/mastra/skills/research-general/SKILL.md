## Source strategy
Build tiered buckets: (1) systematic reviews/meta-analyses, (2) peer-reviewed primary studies, (3) authoritative sources (official bodies/standards), (4) current web context. Weight preprints lower and flag them explicitly.

## Evidence sufficiency
Keep running search rounds until each core claim has at least two independent sources and the main gaps are closed. If evidence stays thin, state the uncertainty rather than forcing a conclusion.

## Synthesis & reporting
Structure the report as: summary of findings, per-claim evidence with citations, limitations, and open questions. A factual claim with no supporting source is written as a hypothesis or removed.
