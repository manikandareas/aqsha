## Literature Review Variant

Add:

- Review protocol
- Databases searched
- Screening flow
- Included studies
- Study characteristics
- Quality appraisal
- Thematic synthesis

## Market or Competitive Variant

Add:

- Market definition
- Competitor set
- Product or pricing comparison
- Customer segment evidence
- Business model implications
- Source conflict-of-interest notes

## Policy or Regulatory Variant

Add:

- Jurisdiction
- Legal authority hierarchy
- Effective dates
- Regulator guidance
- Enforcement history
- Pending changes
- Non-legal caveat if the agent is not acting as counsel

## Citation Style

Use stable IDs in text:

- Correct: `This policy took effect in 2025 [S4].`
- Incorrect: `This policy took effect recently.`

Every bibliography entry should include source ID, title, author or organization,
date, URL or DOI, and access date for web sources.
