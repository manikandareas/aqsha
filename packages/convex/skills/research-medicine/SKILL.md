## Evidence hierarchy
Weight sources by study design: systematic review/meta-analysis > randomized controlled trial (RCT) > cohort > case-control > case series > expert opinion. For intervention-efficacy claims, prefer RCTs and meta-analyses; use cohorts only for exposure/prognosis when an RCT is not ethical/feasible.

## PRISMA screening
Track the identification → screening → eligibility → inclusion flow. Record explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria (population, intervention, comparator, outcome — PICO) and the study count at each stage so the synthesis is traceable.

## Red flags
Flag explicitly: retracted articles (check Retraction Watch/PubPeer), predatory journals (not indexed, fast paid processing without real peer review), industry-funding conflicts of interest, and very small samples. Note statistical discrepancies or high heterogeneity (large I²) rather than discarding them.

## Interpretation
Separate statistical significance from clinical relevance (effect size, NNT/NNH, confidence intervals). Do not extrapolate in-vitro/animal findings to humans without qualification. State uncertainty when evidence is thin rather than forcing a clinical recommendation.
